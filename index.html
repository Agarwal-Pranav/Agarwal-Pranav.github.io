U3Description

The PA-09 is an electrical engineer who has worked at three levels of the stack.

It started with single transistors: a tunnel FET published in Chinese Physics B, and GaN power devices at IISc Bengaluru. It moved up to full chips at IIT Guwahati, taking a design from RTL to GDSII.

It now writes software for the 5G transmit path at Qualcomm.

Off the clock it plays badminton, travels the mountains and is happiest in a crowd.

ANT1Now at Qualcomm

Modem RF SW Engineer · Since July 2026 · Hyderabad

Pranav joined Qualcomm in July 2026 as a Modem RF Software Engineer. He works on the development of the NR Sub-6 GHz transmit path, the 5G bands most phones use every day.

The transmit chain is where a modem's digital data becomes a real radio signal. RF software sets that chain up and keeps it in line: the right band, the right power, and a clean signal that stays inside the spec.
